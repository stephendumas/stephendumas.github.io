## Finding signals from space

My research has examined **radio frequency localization of uncooperative transmitters from space**: using measurements of radio emissions to estimate where a transmitter is located.

The problem brings together several disciplines:

- **Radio frequency engineering and applied electromagnetics** — the signals, propagation, and measurement environment.
- **Signal processing** — extracting useful information from received measurements.
- **Orbital mechanics** — understanding how a spaceborne observer's motion affects the observations.
- **Estimation** — combining the available information to infer transmitter location.
